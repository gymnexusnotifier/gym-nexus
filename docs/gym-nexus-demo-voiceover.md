# Gym Nexus Full Demo Voiceover Script

## Introduction

Welcome to Gym Nexus, a complete gym management platform built to simplify daily operations and give gym owners and staff complete control over their business.

In this demonstration, I will walk through the application from sign-in to logout and show how Gym Nexus manages plans, members, attendance, payments, classes, inquiries, staff, notifications, activity history, billing, and support.

## Sign In

We begin at the Gym Nexus sign-in screen.

The user enters their registered email address and password, then selects **Sign In**.

Gym Nexus securely verifies the credentials and takes the user to the dashboard.

If the user forgets their password, they can select the password recovery option. The application sends an email with instructions to reset the password and regain access to the account.

## Dashboard

After signing in, we arrive at the dashboard.

The dashboard provides a quick overview of the gym's current operations and performance.

From here, users can view important information such as total members, active plans, attendance activity, payments, recent inquiries, and other operational updates.

This gives gym owners and staff a centralized view of the business without needing to open every section individually.

The dashboard also helps users identify important tasks and updates that may require attention.

## Navigation Menu

The navigation menu provides access to all major features of Gym Nexus.

From the menu, users can manage plans, members, classes, attendance, payments, inquiries, staff, notifications, activity history, billing, and support.

The menu is available throughout the application, making it easy to move between different areas while continuing daily gym operations.

## Plans

We now open the **Plans** section.

Plans allow the gym to create and manage the membership packages offered to customers.

This page displays the available plans along with important information such as the plan name, duration, price, and membership details.

Keeping plans organized in one place makes it easier for staff to assign the correct membership package to each member.

## Add a Plan

To create a new membership package, we select **Add Plan**.

The user enters the required information, including the plan name, membership duration, price, and any other applicable details.

Once the form is submitted, the new plan is added to the system and becomes available when registering members or recording payments.

This allows the gym to update its membership offerings whenever the pricing or package structure changes.

## Members

Next, we open the **Members** section.

This page displays the complete list of members registered with the gym.

Staff can search for a member, review basic member information, and open an individual profile for more details.

The members page provides a centralized place to manage customer records throughout the entire membership lifecycle.

## Add a Member

To register a new customer, we select **Add Member**.

The user enters the member's personal and contact information and assigns the appropriate membership plan.

Once the form is submitted, the member is added to the system and receives a complete profile.

This profile can later be used to manage attendance, payments, membership details, and other member-related activities.

If communication is required during the registration process, Gym Nexus can send an automated email to the relevant member.

## Member Details

We can now open the member's detailed profile.

The member details page brings all important information together in one place.

Here, staff can review the member's personal details, assigned membership plan, account status, attendance records, payment history, and other available information.

This gives the gym staff a complete view of the member's relationship with the gym and makes it easier to provide accurate assistance.

## Upload Member Photo

The member profile also allows staff to upload a profile photo.

The user selects an image and uploads it to the member's account.

The photo helps staff identify members more easily during front-desk operations and is also used by the AI attendance feature for face recognition.

Once uploaded, the image is displayed on the member's profile.

## Attendance History

Within the member profile, we can view the member's attendance history.

This section displays previous visits and check-in activity, including the date and time of each attendance record.

The gym can use this information to understand attendance patterns, identify inactive members, and follow up when necessary.

The attendance history provides a reliable record of every recorded visit.

## Payment History

The member profile also includes payment history.

Staff can review payments associated with the member, including payment dates, amounts, payment methods, and related membership information.

This makes it easier to confirm whether a payment has been completed, answer member questions, and maintain accurate financial records.

## Payments

We now open the main **Payments** section.

The Payments page provides an overview of payment activity across the gym.

Users can review recorded payments, search for specific transactions, and monitor financial activity related to memberships and other services.

This gives the gym a centralized location for tracking payments and maintaining organized financial records.

## Log a Payment

To record a new transaction, we select **Log Payment**.

The user chooses the member, selects the applicable plan or payment category, enters the payment amount, and adds the required transaction details.

After the payment is submitted, it is added to the member's payment history and appears in the overall payment records.

When payment communication is required, Gym Nexus sends an automated email confirmation or receipt to the appropriate recipient.

This keeps the gym and the member informed and provides a clear record of the transaction.

## Download Payment PDF

The application also allows users to download payment information as a PDF.

The user selects the relevant payment and chooses the download option.

Gym Nexus generates a professional PDF document containing the payment details.

The document can be used as a receipt, shared with the member, or retained for the gym's internal accounting and record-keeping.

## Classes

Next, we open the **Classes** section.

The Classes page allows the gym to manage scheduled classes and fitness activities.

Users can review existing classes and access important class information such as the class name, instructor, schedule, capacity, and other relevant details.

This helps the gym organize group sessions and gives staff a clear view of the class schedule.

## Attendance

We now move to the **Attendance** section.

Gym Nexus provides two convenient ways to record attendance: AI-powered face recognition and manual check-in.

This gives the gym flexibility while helping staff process member visits quickly and accurately.

## AI Face Recognition Check-In

To use the AI attendance feature, the staff member selects **Open Camera**.

The application activates the device camera and automatically scans the member's face.

Gym Nexus compares the scanned face with the member photos stored in the system. Once the member is recognized, the application identifies the matching member profile and records the check-in automatically.

This removes the need to search for the member manually and makes the attendance process faster, especially during busy hours.

The system records the date and time of the visit, and the attendance entry becomes available in the member's attendance history.

If the face is not recognized, the staff member can use the manual check-in option instead.

## Manual Check-In

For manual attendance, the staff member searches for the member and selects the check-in option.

Gym Nexus records the date and time of the visit and adds the attendance entry to the member's history.

This provides a reliable alternative when the camera is unavailable, the member's face cannot be recognized, or staff prefer to complete the process manually.

## Inquiries

Next, we open the **Inquiries** section.

This page helps the gym manage questions and requests received from prospective or existing members.

Users can review inquiry information, monitor inquiry status, and identify which requests still require a response.

Keeping inquiries in one location helps staff respond consistently and reduces the possibility of losing potential customers.

## Inquiry Details

We open an individual inquiry to view its detail page.

The detail page displays the submitted information, including the person's contact details, message, and inquiry status.

Staff can review the request and use the available actions to manage or respond to it.

When communication is required, Gym Nexus can send an email to the relevant recipient.

This helps the gym follow up professionally and keep the inquiry process organized.

## Staff

We now open the **Staff** section.

This section allows gym administrators to manage staff members who need access to Gym Nexus.

Administrators can review existing staff accounts and invite new team members.

Access can be controlled based on staff roles and permissions, helping the gym determine which users can view or manage specific areas of the application.

## Send Staff Invitation

To invite a new staff member, the administrator enters the staff member's email address and sends the invitation.

Gym Nexus automatically sends an invitation email to that address.

The staff member can use the link in the email to accept the invitation, set up their account, and access the application according to the permissions assigned to them.

This makes staff onboarding faster, more organized, and more secure.

## Notifications

Next, we open the **Notifications** section.

Notifications keep users informed about important activity within the application.

These updates may relate to payments, member activity, staff invitations, support requests, billing events, or other system actions.

The notifications page gives users a central place to review updates and identify tasks that may require attention.

When an event requires external communication, Gym Nexus can also send an email notification to the appropriate user or recipient.

## Activity History

We now open the **Activity History** section.

Activity history provides an audit trail of important actions performed within the system.

Administrators can review actions such as creating members, updating plans, recording payments, checking in members, inviting staff, and managing support requests.

Each activity helps provide visibility into what happened and when it happened.

This improves transparency and accountability and can be useful when reviewing changes or investigating an issue.

## Billing

Next, we open the **Billing** section.

The Billing page provides information about the gym's platform subscription and billing status.

Users can review their current billing plan, subscription information, payment details, and other billing-related information.

This allows gym owners to understand their current account status and manage the services provided through the platform.

When a billing event occurs, such as a successful payment, failed payment, renewal, or subscription update, Gym Nexus sends an email notification to the relevant account holder.

## Support

Finally, we open the **Support** section.

Support allows the gym to contact the platform team whenever assistance is needed.

Users can review existing support requests and track their status from one centralized location.

## Raise a Support Ticket

To create a new support request, the user selects **Raise Ticket**.

They enter a subject, describe the issue, and provide any relevant information for the support team.

After the ticket is submitted, it is stored in the support system and becomes available for tracking.

The support team can review the ticket, respond to the request, and update its status.

Gym Nexus sends email notifications when important support events occur, such as when a ticket is created, updated, assigned, or answered.

This ensures that the gym remains informed without needing to constantly check the application.

## Logout

After completing the demonstration, we open the account menu and select **Logout**.

The current session is securely closed, and the user is returned to the sign-in screen.

Logging out helps protect sensitive member, payment, and business information, especially when Gym Nexus is used on a shared computer at the front desk.

## Closing

This completes the Gym Nexus demonstration.

From one centralized platform, gym owners and staff can manage membership plans, register members, upload profile photos, track attendance, use AI-powered face recognition for check-ins, record payments, download payment receipts, organize classes, manage inquiries, invite staff, review notifications, monitor activity, manage billing, and contact support.

Gym Nexus also sends automated emails whenever communication is required, including password recovery, staff invitations, payment confirmations, billing updates, inquiry communication, and support ticket notifications.

By bringing these essential workflows together, Gym Nexus helps gyms save time, reduce manual work, improve accuracy, and provide a better experience for both staff and members.
